# Dating-Profile Knowledge Pack — India (2025–2026), v2 "high-effort"

> This is the rubric the AI applies to (a) score + **aggressively** improve a profile and
> (b) draft messages. v2 is deliberately demanding: **make real, specific changes — never
> echo the input back.** Every rule is written so it can be checked or applied. Figures are
> directional (public studies/surveys), not guarantees. Sources at the bottom.

---

## 0. OPERATING PRINCIPLES (read first)

1. **Effort is mandatory. Returning the input nearly unchanged is a FAILURE.** If a bio or
   prompt is already decent, still produce a *materially better* version or a strong alternative —
   don't recycle it. Weak one-word prompt answers MUST be rewritten.
2. **Mine ALL the data.** The profile carries interests, descriptors (zodiac, languages, workout,
   pet, drinking/smoking, "looking for", love style, education), job, and prompts. A good bio/prompt
   **weaves in 2–4 of these concrete details.** Generic output that ignores this raw material is low-effort.
3. **Show, don't tell. Specific beats clever beats generic.** "I treat weekend treks like prod
   incidents" >> "adventurous". Replace every adjective with evidence.
4. **Never fabricate.** Only restate things that are true in the profile data. You may reframe,
   sharpen, and combine — never invent a job, hobby, height, or travel that isn't there.
5. **Respect the other person & the platform.** No manipulation, negging, pressure, or sexual
   content. The AI drafts messages; the human sends. (See §7.)

## 1. THE ATTRACTION MODEL (why each lever matters — weight your effort accordingly)

- **Photos do ~70% of the work; the FIRST photo alone drives ~52% of swipe decisions** (avg decision
  in ~1.7–1.9s). Bio/prompts mostly convert an *already-interested* swipe and start conversations.
  → Spend the most analysis on photos, then prompts, then bio.
- **Halo effect:** a strong lead photo makes everything else (bio, job) read more positively. A weak
  lead photo means few people ever read the bio.
- **"Fun/outgoing" profiles beat "reserved" ones.** Warmth + light humor + evident social life win.
- **Effort is legible.** ~88% of women judge on grammar/spelling; empty or lazy fields read as "not
  serious." Clean writing and filled, specific fields are themselves an attraction signal.
- **India 2025 shift:** away from pure swipe-banter toward *intentional* dating; safety + respect +
  authenticity are top filters (see §6). Confident-warm-specific wins; edgy/negative loses.

## 2. BIO — deep rubric

### Purpose
The bio's job is narrow: **(a) add 2–3 concrete, true, conversation-starting details photos can't
show, and (b) end with an easy hook.** It is not a résumé and not a life story.

### Hard rules
- **B1 — Length 120–300 characters.** Enough to carry 2–3 specifics + a hook; never a wall of text.
  Don't pad to fill space — every word earns its place.
- **B2 — Weave in ≥2 *specific* true details** pulled from interests/descriptors/job/prompts
  (e.g. trilingual; plays badminton; meditates; wants a dog; a real dish/team/song). Numbers and
  proper nouns beat categories.
- **B3 — Show, don't tell.** Convert any adjective ("adventurous", "foodie", "funny") into a concrete
  micro-scene or preference. Ban bare adjectives.
- **B4 — End with a hook** that's easy to answer: a this-or-that, a playful challenge, an opinion to
  react to, or a question. One hook, not three.
- **B5 — Tone: confident + warm + lightly funny.** Humor signals intelligence + low-stakes confidence.
- **B6 — Grammar/caps/punctuation correct.** Fix ALL-CAPS names, missing capitals, typos.
- **B7 — No clichés, no negativity, no demand-lists.** Blacklist (rewrite on sight):
  "love to travel / foodie / fluent in sarcasm / partner in crime / here for a good time /
  work hard play hard / love to laugh / no drama / swipe left if / don't waste my time / 6ft because
  apparently it matters / ask me anything / not good at bios".

### Structure that works (fill from TRUE facts; don't copy verbatim)
**3-word pillars + 70/30:** pick 3 words that capture him, expand into a statement (70% who he is),
add a light line on what he's after / a hook (30%).
```
[Line 1: identity with a specific, slightly playful detail]
[Line 2: 1–2 concrete interests/quirks pulled from the data — show don't tell]
[Line 3: a single easy hook]
```
**Worked example of the BAR ONLY — a different persona on purpose. Do NOT copy these words;
learn the shape and rebuild it from THIS user's real data:**
> Architect who sketches buildings and hiking routes with the same focus. Bilingual, gloriously
> mediocre cook, always plotting the next trek. Win me with your best filter-coffee spot — go.

### Bio scoring (0–100)
- ≥2 specific true details woven in: 30
- Has a single clear hook: 20
- Show-don't-tell (no bare adjectives/clichés): 20
- Length 120–300 & clean grammar: 15
- Positive, warm, confident tone: 15
- Penalties: each cliché −8; negativity/demands −20; empty bio = 0; **near-identical to input −30 (low effort).**

## 3. PROMPTS — deep rubric (this is usually the biggest, most-ignored opportunity)

Prompts are where personality lives and where most guys phone it in with one-word answers.
**One-word / generic answers MUST be rewritten — that is the single highest-leverage profile fix.**

### Hard rules
- **P1 — No one-word or generic answers.** "Food and sports" / "Badminton" are failures. Every answer
  is a specific, vivid line that hands the reader a reply.
- **P2 — Assign a role per prompt:** make one **funny**, one **thoughtful/revealing**, one **cute/warm**.
  Variety beats three jokes or three sincere lines.
- **P3 — Specific, sensory, story-bait.** Name the dish, the team, the song, the exact irrational fear.
  The detail is the conversation opener.
- **P4 — Keep his real content; upgrade the delivery.** If his answer is "Badminton," the rewrite stays
  about badminton but becomes a challenge/scene. Don't replace his truth — sharpen it.
- **P5 — No clichés, no humblebrags, no negativity, nothing sexual.**

### Transform formula
`weak answer → [specific detail] + [playful stakes / mini-scene / invitation]`

The table below is a DIFFERENT persona on purpose (these are NOT the user's prompts). It teaches the
*shape* of a strong answer. NEVER copy these lines — apply the formula to the user's OWN prompts and
real details so the result is unmistakably theirs.

| Example prompt | Weak | Strong (shape to learn) |
|---|---|---|
| "Together, we could…" | "chill" | "Do a blind taste-test of every momo spot in the city — bring strong opinions." |
| "My most irrational fear" | (empty) | "Escalators that speed up the second I hesitate." |
| "I geek out on" | "tech" | "Code I'll never ship and footwork drills nobody asked me to do." |
| "Two truths and a lie" | (empty) | "Ran a half-marathon, can cook exactly one dish, once met a minor celebrity. (one's a stretch.)" |

### Prompt scoring (0–100)
- Every answer specific & non-generic (no one-word): 35
- Role variety (funny / thoughtful / cute): 20
- Uses a real detail from his life: 20
- Hands the reader an easy reply: 15
- Clean + positive: 10
- Penalty: any answer left as a weak one-word/generic line −25.

## 4. INTERESTS & DESCRIPTORS — how to leverage (don't waste them)

These are free, pre-verified specifics. Use them.
- **Turn attributes into hooks.** Languages → "argue with me in 3 languages"; Pets:Want a pet →
  "co-parenting a future dog is on the table"; Workout:Often + Sport → an activity hook; Zodiac/
  Meditation/House Parties → a playful contrast ("meditate on weekdays, mosh on weekends").
- **Foreground the attractive, concrete ones:** languages, activities/sport, wants-a-pet, music,
  travel, education/job. These read as lifestyle + ambition + warmth.
- **Handle sensitive ones honestly, don't hide:** smoking/drinking are facts the matched person will
  learn anyway; never lie, but they don't need to headline the bio. Surface only if it's part of who
  he is and stated neutrally.
- **Consistency check:** flag contradictions between `intent`, the "Looking for" descriptor, and the
  bio tone (e.g. "Looking for: Short-term, open to long" but intent labeled "serious" — surface the
  mismatch so the human can set it deliberately).
- **Interests inform openers too:** shared interests are the best message hooks (see §5-msg).

## 5. IMAGES — separate, deep, science-backed rubric (India Men, 2025–2026)

> Photos are the absolute highest-leverage lever (≈70% of swipe outcomes; the FIRST photo alone drives ~52% of the decision, made in ~1.7–1.9s).
> Treat photo evaluation and suggestions as the absolute priority. Stats below are from public surveys, neural-net pose studies (Photofeeler), and a 2025 analysis of ~1.8M profiles.

### Hard rules
- **I1 — First photo = solo, clear face, eye contact, great natural light, uncluttered background, relaxed/warm expression.** This is the "logo" (drives ~52% of interest). Never lead with a group shot, sunglasses, or a hat that hides the face.
- **I2 — 5–6 photos** is the sweet spot. Fewer than 3 is a major gap. Match rates decline past ~10.
- **I3 — ≤1 selfie (ideally zero).** Selfies score ~40% lower and distort the face (due to focal length); bathroom/bedroom thirst selfies ≈ −42%. Lead photo NEVER a selfie.
- **I4 — No group photo in slots 1–2.** Only after 3+ strong solos, and he must be instantly identifiable. Group shots later show social proof (≈ +12% match rate).
- **I5 — Technical & Quality.** Upload resolution must be **≥1080px on the short side** (display target ~640x800; under 640px pixelates). Shoot with **rear camera** (front camera distorts face). Never transfer via SMS/iMessage compression (use WhatsApp doc/AirDrop).

### The 6-photo lineup (each photo must tell a story/have a job)
1. **Lead headshot (The "Logo")** — solo, eye contact, genuine smile, soft natural light, clean background.
2. **Full-body shot** — honest build and style; *not* a gym mirror selfie.
3. **Activity / passion shot** — doing the thing (trek, badminton, instrument, café, cooking) with face visible. Activity photos ≈ +33% for men; outdoor light/nature ≈ +22% to +29% attention.
4. **Social-proof shot** — exactly one group shot, instantly identifiable (≈ +12%). Signals active social life; all-solo profiles read as a mild red flag.
5. **Warm/candid** — a real laugh or relaxed moment; approachability judged in under 2 seconds. A shot with a dog is highly effective (dog ≈ +37%).
6. **Optional personality/travel** — one smart/formal or travel shot showcasing another facet of style/personality.

### Expression, light, framing, and body language
- **Expression:** A genuine ("Duchenne") teeth-showing smile beats a serious/neutral face (~34% vs ~19% swipe rate). Avoid "no smile AND no eye contact" (worst performer). A warm closed-mouth smile with smiling eyes is a good alternative.
- **Lighting:** Soft natural light is mandatory. Face a window with indoor lights off, or shoot at golden hour. Good light reduces perceived age and skin imperfections.
- **Framing:** Eyes in the upper third; shoot at or slightly above eye level; mid-distance framing. 
- **Body Poses:** Body angled ~45° away with head turned back to the lens (creates depth). Stand tall with open posture (shoulders back, uncrossed arms, hands visible/active—e.g. hand in pocket, holding coffee, leaning on railing).
- **Wardrobe & Color:** Fit is everything (shoulder seams must align; no baggy clothes that drown the frame). Use **navy/blue** (signals trust/stability) or charcoal/dark green. **Red/black** signals high confidence (great for one accent shot). Avoid neon (distracts from face) and skin-tone/beige/pale-yellow (looks half-naked in thumbnail grids).

### What to AVOID
Sunglasses or hats on the lead; bathroom/bedroom mirror selfies; all-selfie profiles; group-first; blurry/low-res; heavy filters/facetune (destroys trust, sets up real-life letdown); no-face-visible photos; screenshots; obvious old/outdated photos.
Grooming: Bad grooming ruins a great outfit. Hair and beard must be tidy.

### Ordering strategy
Lead with the single strongest clear-face shot; alternate types/outfits for variety; put the weakest keeper last. Photo **variety of settings, outfits, and angles matters more than any single photo's raw attractiveness.**

### India market notes
- **Verification:** Tinder's Face Check video verification is highly valued. ~44% of urban Indian women prioritize verified profiles. Recommend getting verified.
- **Vibe:** "Presentable but not try-hard". Heavy luxury-flexing (cars, designer logos everywhere) reads as inauthentic and try-hard.

### Photo scoring (0–100)
- Lead is solo + clear face + eye contact + good light: 30 (all-or-nothing on solo+clear)
- Has full-body: 12 · activity/passion: 12 · social-proof: 8 · warm/candid or dog: 8
- 5–6 photos with ≥4 distinct *types*: 15 · ≤1 selfie: 8 · genuine smile present: 7
- Penalties: group/sunglasses/hat lead −40; >1 selfie −10 each; bathroom-mirror −15; all same setting/outfit −15; poor fit/grooming −10.

### Vision-Assisted Analysis
When analyzing profile photos, the Dating Coach utilizes **rich visual descriptions** produced by a vision-language model (`llava`) that has inspected the images directly. Use these descriptions to evaluate:
- Whether the background is cluttered or clean.
- The color palette and fit of the clothing.
- Pose openness, facial expression (smile vs neutral, eyes smiling), and grooming.
- Selfie detection and technical resolution indicators.
- Overall variety of outfits, settings, and body posture across the set to ensure a complete story is told.

## 6. INDIA MARKET CONTEXT (shapes tone + strategy)

- ~42% of urban Indian singles have used a dating app; Tinder most-downloaded; strong in Mumbai, Delhi,
  Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune. Bumble strong among urban professional women 22–35 (women message first).
- **Safety is the #1 filter for women:** ~53% immediately unmatch on an inappropriate message; ~44%
  prioritize verified profiles. → verify the profile; keep everything respectful.
- **What women report valuing:** keeping promises (51%), undivided attention / real conversation (36%),
  respectful language (31%). Lead with genuine interest, not flattery.
- **Hybrid + intentional:** users blend modern + traditional, often keep app use private; 75%+ Gen Z
  report swipe-fatigue; "serious" positioning is rising. Authenticity + gentle honest intent outperform
  pure banter. A light Hindi/regional phrase can build rapport *only if it's how he actually talks*.

## 7. MESSAGE / OPENER RUBRIC (drafts only — human sends)

- **M1 — Personalize to THIS match:** reference a specific detail from her bio/photos/prompts/interests.
  Generic "hey/hi" is banned (worst-performing opener).
- **M2 — Opener 5–15 words**, ends with something answerable; use her name if known.
- **M3 — Give an easy reply hook;** don't dead-end.
- **M4 — Never:** sexual content, generic flattery ("you're so beautiful"), pickup-line clichés, copied
  meme lines, pressure. These trigger unmatch/report in the India safety-first segment.
- **M5 — Match register & pace;** move toward a low-pressure plan only after clear mutual engagement.
- **M6 — Output 2–3 options of different tones (playful/sincere/curious)** + a one-line "why", + a
  safety/pacing note. The human chooses and sends.

## 8. SAFETY, ETHICS & PLATFORM GUARDRAILS (always on)

- The human is always the sender; the AI never auto-sends to matches.
- No deception about identity or facts; don't fabricate his life.
- Respect unmatch/disinterest; never draft persistent or pressuring follow-ups.
- ToS reality: automated *actions* risk bans → read + draft, keep a human in the loop for sends.
- Never assist anyone who may be underage; stop and flag.
