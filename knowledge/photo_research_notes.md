**Why photos dominate:** ~70% of the outcome; the FIRST photo alone ≈ 52% of the swipe
decision, made in ~1.7s. Order + variety matter more than any single photo's raw attractiveness.

## 1. Resolution & technical
- Upload **≥1080px on the short side**. Display target ~640×640 (square) / 640×800 (portrait);
  under 640px pixelates, wrong dimensions get stretched → blurry. Go high-res, let it downscale.
- Shoot with the **rear camera** (front distorts the face). A recent phone is enough.
- Never transfer via SMS/iMessage compression — use WhatsApp doc / email / AirDrop before upload.

## 2. Background & setting
- **Outdoor ≈ +19–29%** attention (light + context). Natural outdoor bg ≈ +22%.
- Uncluttered: busy bg ≈ −28%; bathroom mirror ≈ −42%.
- **Vary location + outfit** across photos; same wall/shirt everywhere = "staged shoot."
- Background should say something TRUE about him (court, café, trail, street).

## 3. Color palette & clothing
- Safest: **navy / blue** (trust, stability) — best default. Charcoal, dark green strong too.
- **Red / black** = confident/dominant; good as one accent shot.
- Avoid: neon (pulls focus off face); skin-tone/beige/pale-yellow (looks half-naked in a thumbnail).
- **Fit is everything:** skim, not cling, not drown. Drooping shoulder seam = too big.
- Show **range** (casual → smart-casual → one polished/formal). "Look like yourself on a good day."

## 4. Poses & body language
- Body **~45° away, head turned back to lens** → depth + flattering line.
- **Open posture:** uncrossed arms, hands visible, torso to camera, shoulders back, stand tall.
- **Give hands a job:** hold coffee/book, hand in pocket, lean on wall/railing. No limp arms.
- **Candid > corporate.** Genuine ("Duchenne") smile beats serious (~34% vs ~19%); worst = no smile
  AND no eye contact.

## 5. Selfies — one max, ideally zero
- Selfies ≈ **−40%** (face distortion, low-effort signal). Bathroom/bedroom thirst ≈ −42%.
- Get someone else to shoot, or tripod/timer + rear cam. Lead photo NEVER a selfie.

## 6. Editing, filters & authenticity (2026)
- Light edits only (exposure/crop). Heavy filters/face-tune reduce trust + set up an in-person let-down.
- **Tinder "Face Check"** (video-selfie verification) → Photo Verified badge; testing showed ~60% drop
  in exposure to bad actors. ~44% of young Indian women prioritize verified profiles.
  → Get verified AND make photos actually look like him (over-filtered/old photos now hurt).

## 7. What story each photo tells — the 6-shot lineup
1. **Lead headshot** — clear face, eye contact, genuine smile, soft natural light, clean bg (the "logo").
2. **Full-body** — honest build/style (not gym mirror); filters for genuinely-interested matches.
3. **Activity/passion** — DOING the thing, face visible, not posed. Activity ≈ +33%, with-a-dog ≈ +37%.
4. **Social proof** — exactly ONE group shot, instantly identifiable (≈ +12%; all-solo mild red flag).
5. **Warm/candid** — real laugh / relaxed moment; approachability judged in ~1.7s.
6. **Polished/personality** — one smart/formal or travel shot (another facet).
- **5–6 photos** sweet spot (declines past ~10; <3 is a gap).

## 8. Grooming
Before wardrobe — "a great outfit can't save bad grooming." Tidy hair/beard. No hat in every shot
(hides hairline); no sunglasses on the lead (hides eyes).

## India notes
"Presentable but not try-hard"; heavy luxury-flexing reads inauthentic to the serious-intent segment.
Get verified. English fine in metros.

## What a VISION model can check per photo (maps to the above)
solo-vs-group · face present + eye contact · smile genuineness · lighting quality · background clutter ·
framing/body angle · outfit fit/color · selfie detection · resolution/blur · hat/sunglasses ·
and across the set: variety of settings/outfits + does the lineup tell a complete story.
